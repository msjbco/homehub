  About HomeHub Insurance Agent Portal
HomeHub Insurance Agent Portal is an Insurance Preparedness & Home Continuity platform. It is not an AMS, CRM, claims system, underwriting tool, or quoting engine.

This platform helps agents provide genuine value to homeowners through preparedness, documentation, and continuity planning — creating stronger client retention through trust rather than transaction.

Version: Prototype Demo Build  ·  Build: May 2026  ·  Platform: HomeHub
  HomeHub does not provide insurance advice, underwriting decisions, legal advice, claims handling, or coverage recommendations. All document sharing is homeowner-controlled and permission-based. Preparedness scores are informational only and do not constitute coverage assessments. Prototype for demonstration purposes — not final pricing, features, or legal terms.
