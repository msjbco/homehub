Getting Started
How do I set up my partner profile?
Go to Settings → Partner Profile. Fill in your organization name, EIN, mission statement, and upload your logo. Your profile is reviewed within 24 hours. Once approved, your partner badge will appear on all homeowner-facing materials.
How do I add homeowners to my network?
From the Network page, click "Add Homeowner" and enter their email address. They will receive an invitation to join HomeHub under your partner umbrella. You can also bulk-import via CSV from the same page.
What is the HomeHub Impact Score?
The Impact Score is a composite metric (0–100) calculated from home safety, documentation completeness, maintenance currency, and community engagement. It updates in real time and can be used in grant applications.
Campaigns
How do I create a new campaign?
Navigate to Campaigns and click "New Campaign." Complete the 4-step form: name and category, target and dates, milestones, and review. Once launched, the campaign appears in your dashboard and homeowners can track progress.
Can I pause or archive a campaign?
Yes. Open the campaign card and use the "..." menu to Pause, Archive, or Duplicate. Paused campaigns retain all data. Archived campaigns appear under the Archived filter tab.
Resources & Branding
Where do I download the HomeHub logo kit?
Under Resources → Branding Kit, you will find logo variations (PNG, SVG, full-color, white), brand color codes, and typography guidelines. All assets are free to use in materials that promote HomeHub.
How do I request custom marketing materials?
Submit a request through Partner Support → Contact Support. Select "Marketing Request" as the type, describe what you need, and our design team will respond within 3 business days.
Billing & Access
Is HomeHub free for nonprofit partners?
Yes. Verified 501(c)(3) organizations receive free partner access including the full NP Dashboard, unlimited campaigns, and community health reports. Enterprise features like API access and white-labeling are available for a fee.
