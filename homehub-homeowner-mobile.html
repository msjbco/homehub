A dirty HVAC filter reduces airflow, strains your system, and can raise energy bills by up to 15%. Replacing it on schedule extends equipment life.
Quick Steps
1
Turn off your HVAC system
Prevents dust from circulating while the filter compartment is open.
2
Locate the filter compartment
Usually in the return air duct or blower compartment. Your filter size: 20×25×1.
3
Remove old filter, insert new one
Arrow on filter should point toward the furnace (away from return air).

Turn system back on & log it
Mark the date on the filter and record it in HomeHub for next reminder.

Help & FAQ

🚀 Getting Started
What is HomeHub and how does it work?
HomeHub is a centralized platform for managing all aspects of your home — projects, maintenance, contractors, warranties, insurance, and historical records. Share secure contractor access via QR codes, track work in real-time, and build a transferable record of your home's history.
How do I get started?
Create an account and add your property info. Explore the Dashboard to see your Home Health Score. Add your first project or set up maintenance reminders. Consider booking Setup Assist for a professional to create a comprehensive baseline.
📋 Projects & Tracking
How do I add a new project?
Go to Projects and tap "+ New Project". Enter the name, description, budget, timeline, and select a contractor. Upload photos and documents, then share with your contractor via a secure QR code.
Can I track multiple projects at once?
Yes! HomeHub allows unlimited simultaneous projects. Organize them by status (Current, Past, Future) or category. Each project has its own timeline, budget, contractor info, and documentation.
🔧 Contractors & Access
How do I share access with contractors?
Go to your project and find the Contractor Access section. Share the QR code with your contractor. They scan it to access that specific project. You control what they see and when access expires.
❤️ Home Health & Maintenance
What is the Home Health Score?
A calculated 0–100 score reflecting the condition of your home's major systems. Based on age, maintenance history, recent repairs, and system status. A higher score may help reduce insurance costs. Improve it through regular maintenance and timely repairs.
How do I set up maintenance reminders?
Go to Maintenance Calendar and add recurring tasks for your home systems. Set frequency (monthly, quarterly, annually) and HomeHub will send alerts before maintenance is due. Weather-triggered alerts are also supported.

Water Shutoff
Step-by-step to locate and turn off your main water supply in an emergency.
Emergency Utilities
Jan 10, 2025

Instructions:

  1. Locate the main water shutoff valve. Typically found in the basement, crawlspace, or utility room near the water meter.
  2. Turn the valve clockwise. Use a wrench if needed. Turn slowly until you feel resistance.
  3. Test to confirm water is off. Open a faucet — if no water flows, the shutoff was successful.
  4. If water still flows, there may be a secondary shutoff at the meter. Call a plumber if you can't locate it.

Important Notes:

  • Know your shutoff location before an emergency occurs.
  • Test the valve annually to ensure it turns smoothly.
  • Keep the area around the valve clear and accessible.

HVAC Filter Replacement
How to replace your HVAC air filters every 3 months to maintain efficiency and air quality.
Maintenance Equipment
Dec 20, 2024

What You'll Need:

  • Replacement air filter (check your system manual for correct size — yours is 20×25×1)
  • Ladder if filter is located high up

Steps:

  1. Turn off your HVAC system.
  2. Locate the filter. Usually in the return air duct or blower compartment.
  3. Note the filter size and airflow arrow direction.
  4. Remove old filter and slide in the new one with the arrow pointing toward the furnace.
  5. Turn system back on.

Schedule:

  • Every 3 months — standard filters with normal use
  • More often if you have pets, allergies, or live in a dusty area

Generator Startup
Quick start guide for your backup generator with critical safety precautions for power outages.
Emergency Equipment
Nov 5, 2024
⚠️ CRITICAL SAFETY WARNING
Always place generator outside in open air. Never run inside — generators produce deadly carbon monoxide. Position at least 20 feet from doors and windows.

Startup Steps:

  1. Move generator outside — at least 20 feet from doors and windows.
  2. Set the fuel valve to "ON".
  3. Set choke to "COLD" (skip if auto-choke).
  4. Turn ignition key to "ON".
  5. Pull starter cord or press electric start.
  6. Let engine warm up 2–3 minutes before connecting devices.
  7. Connect devices one at a time, starting with essentials.

Shutdown Steps:

  1. Turn off all connected devices first.
  2. Wait 2–3 minutes for engine to cool.
  3. Turn ignition key to "OFF".
  4. Set fuel valve to "OFF".
