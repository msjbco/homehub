Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a property to access a project?
Use the "Scan Property" feature in the main menu. The homeowner will provide you with a QR code specific to the project. Your camera will read the code and grant you immediate access to that project's information, photos, notes, and documents. You can only access projects that have been shared with you.
How do I log a completed job?
Click "Log New Job" from the dashboard. Select the property, trade, and date. Describe the problem you found, what work you performed, materials used, and recommendations for future maintenance. Set the urgency level and status. Photos and detailed notes help the homeowner understand the work completed.
What is cost confirmation and when do I do it?
After a job is completed, you and the homeowner confirm the final cost paid. HomeHub tracks this separately from payment processing. Once both parties confirm the cost, both can leave reviews. This creates a transparent record of what was actually spent on each job for the homeowner's accounting purposes.
Can I upload photos and documents to a project?
Yes. When logging a job, you can attach photos of the work performed, before/after comparisons, materials used, and any relevant documentation. Photos help the homeowner see exactly what was done and serve as documentation for insurance claims or future reference. Clear, well-organized photos build trust.
What do the different job statuses mean?
"Completed" means the work is done and ready for final cost confirmation. "Pending Follow-up" indicates additional work may be needed. "Quote Needed" means you're providing an estimate, not completing work yet. The homeowner sees these statuses and uses them to track project progress and plan next steps.
How does the review system work?
After both you and the homeowner confirm the final cost, you can both leave reviews about the work experience. These reviews stay on the homeowner's home record and can be shared as part of their Resale Dossier. Good reviews build your reputation and help homeowners find trusted contractors.
What should I include in maintenance recommendations?
Provide clear guidance on future maintenance: cleaning/filter frequency, expected system lifespan, warning signs to watch for, and suggested timeline for next service. Be specific (e.g., "Replace HVAC filter every 3 months" not "Maintain filter"). This helps homeowners stay ahead of problems and reduces emergency repairs.
How do I track my properties and jobs?
The Properties section shows all homes you have access to via shared QR codes. From each property, you can view pending jobs needing follow-up, active projects, and completed work. The Jobs Dashboard gives you an overview of all pending follow-ups and recent jobs across all properties. This helps you prioritize work.
What are my plan options and what do they include?
Plans vary by subscription level. Basic plans include property access and job logging. Premium plans add advanced features like photo galleries, document management, and scheduled maintenance alerts. Enterprise plans include team management, bulk property access, and priority support. Visit Plans & Billing to see details and upgrade.
Is my information secure and private?
Yes. You only access projects specifically shared with you via QR code. The homeowner controls all access and can revoke it at any time. Your profile information and job history are protected with industry-standard encryption. You never see homeowner information outside the projects they've shared with you.
