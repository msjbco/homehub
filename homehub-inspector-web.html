Executive Summary

This property has been inspected in accordance with professional standards. The home is generally in good condition with proper maintenance evident. Several minor recommendations have been noted below that may require attention within the next 12-24 months.

Detailed Findings

Roof - Good

Asphalt shingles in good condition. Recent maintenance evident. No immediate repairs needed. Recommend routine inspection every 2-3 years.

Foundation - Good

Concrete foundation shows no major cracks or settling. Minor hairline cracks noted but not concerning. Proper grading around structure.

HVAC - Fair

Heating/cooling system is functional but appears to be original (13 years old). Recommend servicing before next cooling season. May require replacement within 5-7 years. Estimated repair/replacement cost: $4,500-$6,500.

Plumbing - Good

Water supply and drainage systems functioning normally. No visible leaks or water damage. Copper pipes in good condition.

Electrical - Fair

Service panel is adequate. Some outlets lack grounding (common in older homes). Recommend having electrician evaluate and upgrade if budget allows. Estimated cost: $2,000-$3,500.

Estimated Repair Costs

HVAC Service/Replacement $4,500 - $6,500
Electrical Upgrades $2,000 - $3,500
Total Estimated $6,500 - $10,000

Inspector Recommendations

  • Priority 1: Have HVAC system professionally serviced before next heating season
  • Priority 2: Consult electrician regarding grounding updates to older outlets
  • Routine Maintenance: Continue regular roof inspections and maintain gutters
